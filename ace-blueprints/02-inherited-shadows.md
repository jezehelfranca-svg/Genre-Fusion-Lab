# Production Blueprint: Inherited Shadows (Heirloom Dirge Blues)

*Generational Burdens & Love — 72 BPM · ≈ 3:47 · 68 bars · first chorus at 0:40*

## 1. Header & Style DNA
- **Title:** Inherited Shadows
- **ACE Layer 4 Context:** This is the most intimate arrangement of the ten, and it is built around one object: a four-note heirloom — E – G – F – E, dotted, low — first heard alone on the slide cello and then literally handed down, cello to harmonica to contralto to baritone. The verses are the parent alone and nearly speaking; the choruses are parent and child together, and in the last chorus the roles quietly reverse so the baritone sings *underneath* the contralto like a roof over her. Every echo in the song grows darker the further it trails — generations — except the last line, which is clean.

  I am keeping the room almost empty until the child arrives. Instrument entry is storytelling: the kit does not appear until the contralto does, the strings wait for Chorus 2, and the choir pad is saved for the final chorus. The reserved escalation is a new vocal block, not a louder fader: the roof. Registers stay strictly separated — the baritone’s floor (E2–E3) never collides with her line (E4 and up).
- **Production Style DNA:** bleak urban blues fusion, 72 BPM slow dragging shuffle, E Phrygian with jazz-blues extensions, four-note slide-cello heirloom motif, upright and sub-bass foundation, processed harmonica, near-spoken baritone, young contralto, darkening ghost echoes, granular-freeze collapse, moonglow convolution reverb

| **Tempo / Rhythmic Feel** | 72 BPM. A slow, dragging shuffle, kick and rim ~25 ms behind the grid, half the beats withheld — a groove that walks like someone who has stopped expecting good news. |
| **Main Engine** | Bedrock: UPRIGHT = Morphine → BAS Upright Bass NUC; SUB = GMS → Pure Bass; CELLO = Sakura → STR Cello FG (Fruity Blood Overdrive → Distructor, metal-slide glissandi); HARP = recorded harmonica (Fruity Squeeze → Frequency Shifter). | **Pads & Textures** | Bed and swell: STALKER = Morphine → ATM Stalker; MOON = GMS → Moon Landing TE; DARK ORCH = FLEX → Dark Orchestra by UVI; CHOIR PAD = Morphine → PAD Catholic Choir MC. The rain bed is STALKER’s noise tail, pitched down and granularised with Gross Beat. MOON blooms as one note per chorus; DARK ORCH enters in Chorus 2; CHOIR PAD is held back for the Final Chorus. |
| **Drums** | Kit: SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx → Slow Beat MC (shuffle body, re-gridded to triplets); METALLIC = Drumaxx → Metallic SPYRO and Industrial 01–06 patterns; drops add Toxic Biohazard → Industrial Hazard ToTc (HAZARD, the strike) and SubWoofer Bomb ToTc (BOMB, the boom). Hit character: a rim-click and a low tom, almost nothing else until the child arrives; one HAZARD strike in the whole song before the solo. |
| **Vocal Treatment** | **Intimate Confessional → Ancestral Echo.** Baritone close, mono, nearly spoken, ValhallaVintageVerb ~8%; contralto 15% left. The **shadow taps** (Frequency Shifter + Vocodex copies delayed 1, 2 and 3 beats, low-passed 6 kHz → 3 kHz → 1.5 kHz) follow the last line of each chorus. Designed power moments: the last line of Chorus 2 and bars 5–8 of the Final Chorus. Ad-libs only in the Final Chorus. |
| **Clarity Strategy** | Vocal Void (2–4 kHz) stays open: Maximus’ 2–4 kHz band on CELLO, HARP, DARK ORCH and MOON is keyed from the vocal bus (−3 to −5 dB while the voice sings). Register map: SUB 30–60 Hz · UPRIGHT 60–200 Hz · CELLO growl 80–250 Hz, cry 600 Hz–1.5 kHz · HARP 400 Hz–1.8 kHz · STALKER 150–800 Hz · CHOIR PAD 300 Hz–1 kHz · MOON and strings air above 5 kHz. Two parts never share a register at the same moment — they alternate in time. The shadow taps are low-passed progressively so they never crowd the vocal void. |

## 3. Section-by-Section Arrangement

> **Instrument key** (exact presets in the Song Map and in the FL Studio Implementation table): UPRIGHT = Morphine BAS Upright Bass NUC · SUB = GMS Pure Bass · CELLO = Sakura STR Cello FG · HARP = recorded harmonica · STALKER = Morphine ATM Stalker · MOON = GMS Moon Landing TE · DARK ORCH = FLEX Dark Orchestra by UVI · CHOIR PAD = Morphine PAD Catholic Choir MC · HAZARD = Toxic Industrial Hazard ToTc · BOMB = Toxic SubWoofer Bomb ToTc · SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx Slow Beat MC · METALLIC = Drumaxx Metallic SPYRO.
>
> **Grid:** one bar = 12 triplet slots (12/8 feel); swung eighths land on the `li` slot. `K` kick · `k` soft kick · `S` snare · `s` ghost snare · `R` rim-click · `T` low tom · `x` hat · `o` open hat · `g` ghost hat · `X` unison stab · `B` upright note · `b` soft upright · `#` sub · `~` sustain · `^` tick · `!` industrial hit · `.` rest. The whole kit sits behind the grid by the amount stated in the Song Map.

### [Intro]
*Bars 1–4 · 0:00–0:13 · energy 2 ▂*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the heirloom motif alone on slide cello, a rain-like noise bed, the sub)

- **Cue:** A **cold, empty hall** with rain somewhere beyond the walls (the STALKER noise bed). SUB holds E1. CELLO bows the four-note heirloom — E – G – F – E, dotted, low — once a bar in ghostly harmonics, with a slow slide into each final E. No drums, no bass: this is the thing being handed down, **naked**. The Convolver is huge and dark.

### [Verse 1]
*Bars 5–12 · 0:13–0:40 · energy 3 ▃*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — a parent notices their child’s small hands and sees their own debts waiting there. Baritone alone, almost spoken.)

- **Cue:** The parent **speaks**, barely sings, close to the microphone. SLOW BEAT gives only a rim-click on 3 every other bar. UPRIGHT plays two notes a bar (E, then B), far behind the beat, with SUB under it. CELLO answers with a pizz-slide that plays the heirloom at the end of bars 4 and 8 — the motif echoing like a family habit. Leave the room **mostly empty**.

- **Rhythm Map:**

### [Chorus 1]
*Bars 13–20 · 0:40–1:07 · energy 5 ▄*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — *I’ll carry it, so you won’t have to.* The contralto sings the hook; the baritone holds a low pedal E3 beneath her.)

- **Cue:** The child’s voice **enters** and the room opens. The contralto sings the descending hook B – A – G – F – E; its last three notes are the heirloom’s, so the cell closes on itself. The baritone does not double her — he **holds the floor**, a low pedal E3. SLOW BEAT’s kick and half-time snare arrive for the first time with hats still silent; UPRIGHT and SUB sit on long roots; one MOON pad note blooms on the Fmaj7♯11 in bars 3 and 7. Chords: Am7 | G7 | Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9, twice. On the last line, three shadow taps trail her voice, each darker than the last — generations behind.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text

### [Post-Chorus Tag]
*Bars 21–24 · 1:07–1:20 · energy 4 ▃*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — CELLO plays the heirloom once, HARP hums back a fifth higher, the kick holds the pulse)

- **Cue:** **Four bars, one phrase.** CELLO states E – G – F – E; HARP answers it a fifth higher, breathy; the kick holds the pulse and nothing else moves. It should loop as a 15-second **lullaby with a bruise**.

### [Verse 2]
*Bars 25–32 · 1:20–1:47 · energy 4 ▃*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — what he never said aloud: the ledger of mistakes he keeps hidden. In bars 5–8 the contralto hums the heirloom a third above — the child half-hearing.)

- **Cue:** A brushed, ghost-hat shuffle. HARP plays the heirloom in bars 2 and 6, thin and breathy. The baritone speaks his hidden ledger; in bars 5–8 the **contralto hums** the motif a third above — the child half-hearing. The rain bed returns, louder.

### [Chorus 2]
*Bars 33–40 · 1:47–2:13 · energy 6 ▅*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the hook again with both voices; the baritone drops a fifth below the contralto for bars 5–8.)

- **Cue:** A fuller shuffle: hats open, snare on 3. DARK ORCH enters **low and slow** on the Fmaj7♯11 in bars 3 and 7. The baritone drops a fifth below the contralto for the second half. On bar 5, beat 1: Industrial drop #1 — a single heavy HAZARD strike with a BOMB boom, the debt landing on someone else. Shadow taps follow both voices on the last line. Power moment: the last line.

### [Bridge — The Handing-Down]
*Bars 41–46 · 2:13–2:33 · energy 3 ▃*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — hummed, no words: the motif passed from voice to voice. Contralto, then baritone, then both.)

- **Cue:** The **thematic centre**. SUB only. Bar 1: the heirloom on CELLO. Bar 2: the same four notes on HARP. Bar 3: hummed by the contralto. Bar 4: hummed by the baritone. Bars 5–6: all four play it together in a loose canon, one beat apart. No drums; each handoff carries a shadow tap that fades before the next. Chords: Cmaj7 | Dm7 | Em7♭9 | Em7♭9 | Fmaj7 | Em7♭9.

- **Rhythm Map:**

HARP:        .       motif   .       .       motif   motif   (one beat after the cello)

SUB:         #       #       #       #       #       #
SHADOW TAP:  tap     tap     tap     tap     taps    taps    (each fades before the next handoff)
```

### [Solo — Intimate → Wave]
*Bars 47–54 · 2:33–3:00 · energy 3→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — pizz-slide and harmonics, a low-tom heartbeat, rising to a wave)

- **Cue:** **Bars 1–4:** CELLO pizz-slide and bowed harmonics alone with SUB and the rain bed; HARP sustains long notes from bar 3. **Bars 5–8:** CELLO bows and climbs to the high register; DARK ORCH enters on bar 5 with a slow wail rising a minor sixth; a low-tom heartbeat (SLOW BEAT) on 1 and 3 from bar 6, no hats; MOON opens from bar 6; a wordless spectral choir sings the heirloom on ‘ah’ from bar 7. Bar 8: Industrial drop #2 at the peak.

- **Rhythm Map:**

### [Collapse — The Photograph]
*Bars 55–56 · 3:00–3:07 · energy 1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — C5 granular freeze: the last chord held as a grain cloud, then silence)

- **Cue:** Everything stops except **one frozen chord** — the wave’s last Fmaj7♯11 caught in a grain cloud (Gross Beat time-hold, Convolver at 100% wet), like a photograph of the family. In bar 2 it slowly decays over the sub. Then silence.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar 1:        ALL STEMS    [STOP on beat 1 — everything cuts except the frozen chord]
GRAIN CLOUD:  Fmaj7♯11     [HELD, 100% wet, full width — the photograph]
Bar 2:        ALL STEMS    [the cloud decays over SUB for 4 beats, then COMPLETE SILENCE]
```

### [Final Chorus]
*Bars 57–64 · 3:07–3:33 · energy 5→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the parent steps aside, then shelters her. Contralto alone, then the baritone an octave below, then the full wave. Ad-libs permitted here only.)

- **Cue:** The roles **reverse**. Bars 1–2: the kit is silent and the contralto sings alone over the sub — the parent steps aside. Bars 3–4: the baritone enters an octave below her — **the roof** — and the shuffle returns. Bars 5–8: the full wave — both voices, full kit, CELLO in its cry register, DARK ORCH wailing, CHOIR PAD swelling for the first time. This is the reserved lift: a new vocal block. The last line is sung with no shadow taps on her voice; they follow the baritone, not her. No G♯ anywhere.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar:         1        2        3        4        5        6        7        8
CONTRALTO:   hook     hook     hook     hook     HOOK     HOOK     HOOK     LAST LINE (no taps on her)
BARITONE:    .        .        roof     roof     roof     roof     roof     roof (taps follow him)
SLOW BEAT:   [SILENT] [SILENT] shuffle  shuffle  FULL     FULL     FULL     FULL
CELLO:       .        .        .        .        CRY      CRY      CRY      CRY
DARK ORCH:   .        .        .        .        WAIL     WAIL     WAIL     WAIL
CHOIR PAD:   .        .        .        .        swell    swell    swell    swell   (first appearance)
```

### [Outro]
*Bars 65–68 · 3:33–3:47 · energy 2 ▂*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — HARP plays the heirloom once, clean, an octave and a fifth higher; the sub holds)

- **Cue:** The child **receives** it. HARP plays E – G – F – E once, an octave and a fifth higher, clean — no slide, no bend. CELLO’s low-E drone fades under it and SUB holds to the end. The reverb tail is cut short: the song ends mid-air, not in a hall.
