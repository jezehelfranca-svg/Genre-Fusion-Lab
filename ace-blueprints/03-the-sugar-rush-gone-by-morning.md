# Production Blueprint: The Sugar Rush (Gone by Morning) (Comedown Shuffle Noir)

*Fleeting Comfort vs. Lasting Peace — 90 BPM · ≈ 3:49 · 86 bars · first chorus at 0:48*

## 1. Header & Style DNA
- **Title:** The Sugar Rush (Gone by Morning)
- **ACE Layer 4 Context:** A rush and its crash. The first two choruses are the densest, brightest, bounciest thing this style allows: a pad that pumps hard against the kick, industrial drops arriving like *hits*, each followed by a bit-crushed comedown a beat and a half long. The tempo is the top of the range, so the drag is nearly gone — the groove is almost keeping up with the buzz. Then a collapse, a hollow 3 a.m. bridge, and a solo that starts to swell like a sunrise and is cut off at its peak.

  The final chorus is the same hook with everything the rush built taken away: no drums, no pump, no choir, only upright, sub, one cello line and two voices. In my arrangement the escalation I normally reserve for the last chorus is **withheld** — the denial is the lift. Register discipline matters even in the chaos: the pump is keyed from the kick only, never from the voices, so the vocal void stays open.
- **Production Style DNA:** bleak urban blues fusion, 90 BPM jittery triplet shuffle, E Phrygian with jazz-blues extensions, sidechain-pumped synth pad, bit-crushed comedowns after industrial hits, bouncing upright and sub-bass, distorted slide cello, processed harmonica, raw baritone and contralto duet, moonglow convolution reverb, stripped-bare final chorus

## 2. Song Map Table

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Key** | E Phrygian (home chord Em7♭9). The half-step F is hit constantly, like a nervous tic. E7♭9 appears as a brief bright flash at the end of choruses 1–2 only — the sugar spike — and never again. |
| **Tempo / Rhythmic Feel** | 90 BPM, the fastest shuffle in the set: bouncy triplet hats, kick and snare only ~12 ms behind the grid. The drag is nearly gone, which is exactly the problem. |
| **Main Engine** | Bedrock: UPRIGHT = Morphine → BAS Upright Bass NUC; SUB = GMS → Pure Bass; CELLO = Sakura → STR Cello FG (Fruity Blood Overdrive → Distructor, metal-slide glissandi); HARP = recorded harmonica (Fruity Squeeze → Frequency Shifter). UPRIGHT bounces root – ♭9 – root; CELLO doubles the hook with fuzz; HARP locks to the hats. |
| **Pads & Textures** | Bed and swell: STALKER = Morphine → ATM Stalker; MOON = GMS → Moon Landing TE; DARK ORCH = FLEX → Dark Orchestra by UVI; CHOIR PAD = Morphine → PAD Catholic Choir MC. MOON and DARK ORCH are the pumping layers (Maximus sidechain keyed from the kick); STALKER is the 3 a.m. room; CHOIR PAD is not used in the final chorus at all. |
| **Drums** | Kit: SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx → Slow Beat MC (shuffle body, re-gridded to triplets); METALLIC = Drumaxx → Metallic SPYRO and Industrial 01–06 patterns; drops add Toxic Biohazard → Industrial Hazard ToTc (HAZARD, the strike) and SubWoofer Bomb ToTc (BOMB, the boom). Hit character: bright, tight, jittery; hats rush in triplets, the Industrial hits are short and dry because the comedown does the damage. |
| **Vocal Treatment** | **Breathless Duet → Bare Duet.** Baritone and contralto trade lines fast, close and mostly dry, ValhallaVintageVerb ~10%; octave unison on the hook, a fourth-above split in Chorus 2. The spectral choir (Frequency Shifter + Vocodex) appears only in the post-chorus drop and the solo. The final chorus is dry unison, no harmony and no choir. Designed power moment: Chorus 2, bar 5. Ad-libs only in Chorus 2’s second half (the one place the rush may shout). |
| **Clarity Strategy** | Vocal Void (2–4 kHz) stays open: Maximus’ 2–4 kHz band on CELLO, HARP, DARK ORCH and MOON is keyed from the vocal bus (−3 to −5 dB while the voice sings). Register map: SUB 30–60 Hz · UPRIGHT 60–200 Hz · CELLO growl 80–250 Hz, cry 600 Hz–1.5 kHz · HARP 400 Hz–1.8 kHz · STALKER 150–800 Hz · CHOIR PAD 300 Hz–1 kHz · MOON and strings air above 5 kHz. Two parts never share a register at the same moment — they alternate in time. The pump is keyed from the kick only (Maximus sidechain ~6 dB, fast attack, ~120 ms release), so it never ducks the voice. |

## 3. Section-by-Section Arrangement

> **Instrument key** (exact presets in the Song Map and in the FL Studio Implementation table): UPRIGHT = Morphine BAS Upright Bass NUC · SUB = GMS Pure Bass · CELLO = Sakura STR Cello FG · HARP = recorded harmonica · STALKER = Morphine ATM Stalker · MOON = GMS Moon Landing TE · DARK ORCH = FLEX Dark Orchestra by UVI · CHOIR PAD = Morphine PAD Catholic Choir MC · HAZARD = Toxic Industrial Hazard ToTc · BOMB = Toxic SubWoofer Bomb ToTc · SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx Slow Beat MC · METALLIC = Drumaxx Metallic SPYRO.
>
> **Grid:** one bar = 12 triplet slots (12/8 feel); swung eighths land on the `li` slot. `K` kick · `k` soft kick · `S` snare · `s` ghost snare · `R` rim-click · `T` low tom · `x` hat · `o` open hat · `g` ghost hat · `X` unison stab · `B` upright note · `b` soft upright · `#` sub · `~` sustain · `^` tick · `!` industrial hit · `.` rest. The whole kit sits behind the grid by the amount stated in the Song Map.

### [Cold Open]
*Bars 1–2 · 0:00–0:05 · energy 5 ▄*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — one industrial hit, then MOON flashes the six-note cell as a bright filter-sweep line)

- **Cue:** The song starts **mid-rush**, as if the first hit already happened. Bar 1, beat 1: a single HAZARD strike; triplet hats start on beat 2. MOON sweeps its filter open over Em7♭9 while it plays the six-note cell high and fast — B – A – G – F – E – B — a flash of the hook before anyone sings.

### [Intro]
*Bars 3–6 · 0:05–0:16 · energy 4 ▃*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the shuffle at full speed; upright bounces; harmonica chugs in sync with the hats)

- **Cue:** A full-speed shuffle, **jittery and bright**: kick on 1 and the ‘a’ of 2, snare on 3, triplet hats. UPRIGHT bounces root – ♭9 – root; HARP chugs the shuffle pattern bit-crushed, locked to the hats. Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11, twice.

### [Verse 1]
*Bars 7–14 · 0:16–0:37 · energy 5 ▄*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the buzz of the quick fix, the thing that takes the ache away tonight. Baritone, fast and slightly breathless.)

- **Cue:** Not sparse but **tight**: the snare moves to 2 and 4 and the hats ghost. UPRIGHT walks quickly with a short stutter on the last beat of each bar; HARP stabs two-note E – F figures on the ‘li’ of 3. The baritone is fast and a little breathless, close and dry. One micro-glitch — a 1/16 Gross Beat buffer repeat — on the last beat of bar 8. Chords: Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11, twice.

### [Pre-Chorus 1]
*Bars 15–18 · 0:37–0:48 · energy 6 ▅*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the voices tumble over each other, alternating lines every half-bar.)

- **Cue:** Hat triplets double into 16th-triplets across bars 3–4 and a snare roll builds on bar 4; MOON’s pitch rises a minor sixth across the four bars. Both voices alternate lines every half-bar, **tumbling**. HARP rattles the cell over Am7 | Bm7♭5 | Cmaj7 | Dm7. Bar 4, beat 4: Industrial drop #1 (HAZARD + BOMB).

### [Chorus 1]
*Bars 19–26 · 0:48–1:09 · energy 8 ▆*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — it’s sweet right now; don’t ask about tomorrow. Octave unison, bright and upfront.)

- **Cue:** The rush **peaks** — bright, wide, pumping. Bar 5, beat 1: Industrial drop #2 — followed by the first **comedown**, Fruity Squeeze plus low-pass for a beat and a half, then recovery. Chords: Em7♭9 | G7 | Fmaj7♯11 | E7♭9, twice — the E7♭9 is a brief bright flash.

### [Post-Chorus Drop]
*Bars 51–54 · 2:13–2:24 · energy 9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — four-on-the-floor stomp, wordless spectral choir echoing the hook)

- **Cue:** **The peak of the rush**: a four-on-the-floor kick stomp over the shuffle for four bars, a wordless spectral choir echoing the hook as pure sound. On bar 4 the whole mix starts to pitch down and bit-crush in preparation for the crash.

### [Crash — 3 A.M.]
*Bars 55–58 · 2:24–2:35 · energy 4→1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — C3 bit-crush dropout; near silence, a slow dry tick)

- **Cue:** Fruity Squeeze degrades every stem to low-bit and low sample rate over bars 1–2, then drops out. Bars 3–4: near silence — SUB, the reverb tail and a very slow, dry clock-like tick (METALLIC). **The sugar is gone.**

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar:          1                  2                  3                 4
ALL STEMS:    [BIT-CRUSH 8→3]    [DROPOUT]          [NEAR SILENCE]    [NEAR SILENCE]
SUB:          #                  #~~                ~                 ~
TICK:         .                  .                  ^ (slow, dry)     ^ (slow, dry)
```

### [Comedown Bridge]
*Bars 59–66 · 2:35–2:56 · energy 2 ▂*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the stale room at 3 a.m., the quiet after the sugar. The contralto alone, low and tired; the baritone joins in bar 5 a third below.)

- **Cue:** The **stale room**. A half-time kick on 1 only, no hats; UPRIGHT one note per bar; CELLO bowed harmonics, very slowly, quoting the first three notes of the cell (B – A – G). The contralto sings alone, low and tired; the baritone joins in bar 5 a third below. The Convolver is wetter here than anywhere else in the song. Chords: Em7♭9 pedal, Fmaj7♯11 on bars 4 and 8.

### [Solo — False Sunrise]
*Bars 67–74 · 2:56–3:17 · energy 3→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — slide cello alone, then a sunrise-wave that is cut off at its peak)

- **Cue:** **Bars 1–4:** CELLO alone, high and tender; HARP answers softly from bar 3. **Bars 5–8:** SLOW BEAT returns (kick and rim); MOON opens from bar 5 — the sunrise; DARK ORCH wails from bar 6; a wordless spectral choir from bar 7; CELLO climbs into a full cry. On bar 8, beat 3: **C4 vacuum cut** — a reversed-reverb swell is sucked in, then everything stops dead. It must not resolve.

### [Final Chorus — Gone by Morning]
*Bars 75–82 · 3:17–3:39 · energy 3 ▃*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the rush is a stranger now. The same hook sung close and nearly dry by both voices in unison; no harmony, no choir.)

- **Cue:** The same hook with **everything removed**: no drums, no synths, no strings, no pump, no choir, no industrial drops. UPRIGHT and SUB on long notes, one low CELLO line without fuzz, both voices in unison, close and nearly dry. Em7♭9 | G7 | Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9, twice — no E7♭9 flash. The reserved escalation is **withheld**: the denial is the lift. The room is the same size; nobody is in it.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bars 1–8 (every bar):
UPRIGHT:      B.....B.....        (long roots, no pushed note)
SUB:          #~~~~~~~~~~~
CELLO:        one low line, no fuzz
DRUMS / SYNTHS / STRINGS / CHOIR / PUMP / INDUSTRIAL:   NONE
```

### [Outro]
*Bars 83–86 · 3:39–3:49 · energy 1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — sub fades, a slow tick, the dry room)

- **Cue:** SUB fades; a single slow tick; the last bar is dry room tone and the end of the reverb tail. **Gone by morning.**
