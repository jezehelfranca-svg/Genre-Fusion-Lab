# Production Blueprint: Planting Seeds You Won’t See Grow (Hollow-Congregation Call-and-Response Blues)

*Human Capital & Mentorship — 76 BPM · ≈ 3:54 · 74 bars · first chorus at 0:38*

## 1. Header & Style DNA
- **Title:** Planting Seeds You Won’t See Grow
- **ACE Layer 4 Context:** A teaching song, so I arranged it as call and response. The baritone calls; the contralto answers. At first she is an echo with small mistakes, and in the solo the harmonica plays her first halting steps. As the song grows the voices multiply — one, two, four, then a spectral congregation of eight or more, each copy slightly different in pitch and timing, like a roomful of different students. The final chorus swells into the most radiant wave of the ten and then **hangs on E7♭9, unresolved**: the mentor does not hear the answer. Someone else will.

  The gospel lift here is a choir-pad swell — one bar of crescendo, instant fall — not a pipe organ, and the handclaps arrive only when the congregation does. Instrument entry follows the voice count. The reserved escalation is the full backing-vocal block in the final chorus; before that the room is deliberately emptier than the theme suggests, because a field has to look bare before it can bloom.
- **Production Style DNA:** bleak urban blues fusion, 76 BPM rolling dragging shuffle with a gospel lilt, E Phrygian with jazz-blues extensions, baritone calls and contralto answers, voices growing to a ghost congregation, choir-pad swells, processed handclaps, halting harmonica, distorted slide cello, vacuum-cut collapse, moonglow convolution reverb

Two parts never share a register at the same moment — they alternate in time. Spectral-choir copies are spread wide and kept under 1 kHz around the leads; handclaps are high-passed at 800 Hz and ducked from the vocal bus. |

> **Instrument key** (exact presets in the Song Map and in the FL Studio Implementation table): UPRIGHT = Morphine BAS Upright Bass NUC · SUB = GMS Pure Bass · CELLO = Sakura STR Cello FG · HARP = recorded harmonica · STALKER = Morphine ATM Stalker · MOON = GMS Moon Landing TE · DARK ORCH = FLEX Dark Orchestra by UVI · CHOIR PAD = Morphine PAD Catholic Choir MC · HAZARD = Toxic Industrial Hazard ToTc · BOMB = Toxic SubWoofer Bomb ToTc · SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx Slow Beat MC · METALLIC = Drumaxx Metallic SPYRO.
>
> **Grid:** one bar = 12 triplet slots (12/8 feel); swung eighths land on the `li` slot. `K` kick · `k` soft kick · `S` snare · `s` ghost snare · `R` rim-click · `T` low tom · `x` hat · `o` open hat · `g` ghost hat · `X` unison stab · `B` upright note · `b` soft upright · `#` sub · `~` sustain · `^` tick · `!` industrial hit · `.` rest. The whole kit sits behind the grid by the amount stated in the Song Map.

- **Lyrics:**

### [Chorus 2]
*Bars 33–40 · 1:41–2:06 · energy 7 ▆*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the hook again with four voices: both leads and two ghost harmonies in thirds.)

- **Cue:** Four voices now — both leads plus two ghost harmonies in thirds — and the spectral choir takes the whole last line. Full shuffle plus **handclaps** on 2 and 4 (METALLIC claps doubled with the rim). CHOIR PAD swells on bars 4 and 8; MOON opens; DARK ORCH wails in from bar 5, long and slow. Bar 8, beat 4: Industrial drop #1 (HAZARD steel-pipe strike) at the swell’s peak — and the next section cuts it dead. Power moment: the last line.

### [Collapse — Winter Field]
*Bars 41–44 · 2:06–2:19 · energy 8→1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — C4 vacuum cut: the swell pulled into a single point, then silence)

- **Cue:** **A winter, not a crash.** Bars 1–3: a reversed-reverb swell pulls everything inward to a single point while the filter closes. Bar 4: a hard cut to silence with only the Convolver tail — the field with nothing visible in it yet.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bars 1–3:   ALL STEMS    [REVERSED-REVERB SWELL pulls everything inward to one point; filter closes]
Bar 4:      ALL STEMS    [HARD CUT → COMPLETE SILENCE — Convolver tail only]
```

### [Bridge — You Won’t See It]
*Bars 45–50 · 2:19–2:38 · energy 2 ▂*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — the quiet knowledge that the harvest will belong to someone else. The contralto alone, near-spoken, over the sub; the baritone joins in bar 4 a third below.)

- **Cue:** No drums. SUB holds one long E. The contralto, close to speech, sings what the mentor already knows. At bar 4 the baritone joins a third below. At bar 5 the MOON seed tone drifts back in. Chords: Cmaj7 | Dm7 | Em7♭9 | Em7♭9 | Fmaj7 | Em7♭9.

### [Solo — The Student’s First Steps]
*Bars 51–58 · 2:38–3:03 · energy 3→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — halting harmonica growing surer, joined by cello, swelling into a wave)

- **Cue:** **Bars 1–2:** HARP, raw and close, halting — E – F – E, then silence: the first steps. **Bars 3–4:** a longer, surer phrase — the full call, E – F – G – B. **Bars 5–8:** CELLO joins in a high cry and the two trade phrases; a low-tom heartbeat (SLOW BEAT) from bar 5, the shuffle by bar 7; MOON and CHOIR PAD from bar 6, DARK ORCH wailing from bar 6; a wordless spectral choir on bar 7. Bar 8: Industrial drop #2 at the peak. Chords: Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9 | Dm7, twice.

### [Final Chorus — The Bloom]
*Bars 59–66 · 3:03–3:28 · energy 8→10 █*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — you’ll never see it grow, sung by a congregation. Eight or more voices; the contralto’s answer is clean for the first time. Ad-libs permitted here only.)

- **Cue:** The **congregation**. Eight or more voices: both leads, ghost harmonies and the spectral choir singing the hook — each copy varied by a few cents and tens of milliseconds so it sounds like different people. The contralto sings the answer **without error** for the first time. Full kit with handclaps and a crash on every bar 1; CHOIR PAD swells on bars 4 and 8; DARK ORCH wails at full strength; CELLO in its cry register. This is the reserved lift: the full backing-vocal block. Ad-libs permitted here and nowhere else.

### [Bloom Tag — E7♭9]
*Bars 67–70 · 3:28–3:41 · energy 10 █*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the unresolved chord held in a wave; no words)

- **Cue:** **The bloom.** The progression stops on E7♭9 and holds for four bars at full wave: the spectral choir sustains the high B and the G♯, CHOIR PAD and DARK ORCH swell and fall in waves, MOON and CELLO wail around the chord. Industrial drops on bars 1 and 3. On bar 4 the drums drop out and the held chord decays — **no resolution to Am or Em**.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar:          1        2        3        4
CHORD:        E7♭9     E7♭9     E7♭9     E7♭9 (decays — NO resolution to Am or Em)
SPECTRAL:     B + G♯   B + G♯   B + G♯   fading
CHOIR PAD:    swell    fall     swell    fall
DARK ORCH:    wail     wail     wail     fade
MOON / CELLO: wail     wail     wail     fade
DRUMS:        full     full     full     [DROP OUT]
HAZARD+BOMB:  !        .        !        .
```

### [Outro]
*Bars 71–74 · 3:41–3:54 · energy 2 ▂*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the seed tone returns and climbs to a lone G♯)

- **Cue:** The seed tone returns — MOON’s soft E4 — then climbs F – G and comes to rest on a lone **G♯**, the bright note, unanswered. A single breath of spectral choir, then silence. The harvest belongs to someone else.
