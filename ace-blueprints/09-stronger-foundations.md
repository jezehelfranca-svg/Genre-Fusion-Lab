# Production Blueprint: Stronger Foundations (Bedrock-Up Blues)

*Craftsmanship & Enduring Love — 78 BPM · ≈ 3:48 · 74 bars · first chorus at 0:49*

## 1. Header & Style DNA
- **Title:** Stronger Foundations
- **ACE Layer 4 Context:** Architecture. The song is built literally from the ground up: the intro is just sub and upright laying a single stone, and every following section adds the next register higher — a plucked cello figure, then drums, then harmonica, then pads and strings, then the full voices and the wave. A bright, fast, flashy tower — **the Sand** — tries to skip the work: all high frequencies and no foundation. It collapses in two bars. The solo rebuilds from the floor, in the same order but slower, and the final chorus is a cathedral: every register, a wide wave, and the E pedal never moving.

  This is the arrangement where register discipline *is* the story. Every chord is voiced over an E pedal, every added instrument has its own neighbourhood, and the escalation I reserved — the full-range strings block — is spent exactly once, in the final chorus, because a building only gets one roof. The last sound is the bedrock: upright and sub on a bare fifth.
- **Production Style DNA:** bleak urban blues fusion, 78 BPM steady sturdy shuffle, E Phrygian with jazz-blues extensions, built bottom-up over an unmoving E pedal, plucked cello fingerpicked figure, upright and sub-bass foundation, flashy high-register tower collapsing like sand, wide unhurried final wave, moonglow convolution reverb, raw baritone and contralto in fifths

## 2. Song Map Table

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Key** | E Phrygian (home chord Em7♭9). Every chord is voiced **over an E pedal** (Fmaj7♯11/E, Am7/E, Cmaj7/E, Dm7/E) because bedrock does not move. The pedal is removed only in the Sand, where there is no foundation. |
| **Tempo / Rhythmic Feel** | 78 BPM. A steady, slow, sturdy 12/8 shuffle, ~15 ms behind the grid — the least drag of the slow half, because this song is *on* its foundation. |
| **Main Engine** | Bedrock: UPRIGHT = Morphine → BAS Upright Bass NUC; SUB = GMS → Pure Bass; CELLO = Sakura → STR Cello FG (Fruity Blood Overdrive → Distructor, metal-slide glissandi); HARP = recorded harmonica (Fruity Squeeze → Frequency Shifter). UPRIGHT and SUB are the foundation and play first; CELLO plucks the rolling fingerpicked figure E – B – G – B – E – B – G – B. |
| **Pads & Textures** | Bed and swell: STALKER = Morphine → ATM Stalker; MOON = GMS → Moon Landing TE; DARK ORCH = FLEX → Dark Orchestra by UVI; CHOIR PAD = Morphine → PAD Catholic Choir MC. Built bottom-up: STALKER (Chorus 1, low-mid) → HARP (Verse 2) → MOON and DARK ORCH (Chorus 2) → CHOIR PAD (Final Chorus). In the Sand, MOON becomes a bright arpeggio. |
| **Drums** | Kit: SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx → Slow Beat MC (shuffle body, re-gridded to triplets); METALLIC = Drumaxx → Metallic SPYRO and Industrial 01–06 patterns; drops add Toxic Biohazard → Industrial Hazard ToTc (HAZARD, the strike) and SubWoofer Bomb ToTc (BOMB, the boom). Hit character: deep, plain, unadorned — kick and rim first, hats third. The industrial drops are single anvil strikes: stones being set down. |
| **Vocal Treatment** | **Stable Fifths → Cathedral.** Baritone close, warm, mono; contralto 15% left, singing in fifths and octaves (E3 / B3, then E4). The spectral choir grows 2 → 4 → full width; the voices never leave the stable intervals. Designed power moments: Chorus 2’s last line and the Final Chorus’s hook. Ad-libs only in the Final Chorus. |
| **Clarity Strategy** | Vocal Void (2–4 kHz) stays open: Maximus’ 2–4 kHz band on CELLO, HARP, DARK ORCH and MOON is keyed from the vocal bus (−3 to −5 dB while the voice sings). Register map: SUB 30–60 Hz · UPRIGHT 60–200 Hz · CELLO growl 80–250 Hz, cry 600 Hz–1.5 kHz · HARP 400 Hz–1.8 kHz · STALKER 150–800 Hz · CHOIR PAD 300 Hz–1 kHz · MOON and strings air above 5 kHz. Two parts never share a register at the same moment — they alternate in time. The E pedal (SUB + UPRIGHT) is the loudest steady low element throughout. In the Sand it is removed and everything is high-passed at ~400 Hz, so the missing foundation is audible. |

## 3. Section-by-Section Arrangement

> **Instrument key** (exact presets in the Song Map and in the FL Studio Implementation table): UPRIGHT = Morphine BAS Upright Bass NUC · SUB = GMS Pure Bass · CELLO = Sakura STR Cello FG · HARP = recorded harmonica · STALKER = Morphine ATM Stalker · MOON = GMS Moon Landing TE · DARK ORCH = FLEX Dark Orchestra by UVI · CHOIR PAD = Morphine PAD Catholic Choir MC · HAZARD = Toxic Industrial Hazard ToTc · BOMB = Toxic SubWoofer Bomb ToTc · SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx Slow Beat MC · METALLIC = Drumaxx Metallic SPYRO.
>
> **Grid:** one bar = 12 triplet slots (12/8 feel); swung eighths land on the `li` slot. `K` kick · `k` soft kick · `S` snare · `s` ghost snare · `R` rim-click · `T` low tom · `x` hat · `o` open hat · `g` ghost hat · `X` unison stab · `B` upright note · `b` soft upright · `#` sub · `~` sustain · `^` tick · `!` industrial hit · `.` rest. The whole kit sits behind the grid by the amount stated in the Song Map.

### [Intro — The First Stone]
*Bars 1–8 · 0:00–0:25 · energy 2 ▂*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — sub and upright alone, one note at a time, laying the first stone)

- **Cue:** **One note at a time.** SUB and UPRIGHT alone: bars 1–4 one long E per two bars; bars 5–8 the upright pushes the cell — E – B – E – F – E — out as slow long tones, then rests. - **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — what lasts is quiet. The baritone sings bars 1–4, the contralto bars 5–8: sharing the load.)

### [Chorus 2]
*Bars 37–44 · 1:51–2:15 · energy 6 ▅*

- **Lyrics:**

- **Cue:** Full shuffle with the snare on 3 and open hats. MOON opens wide with a slow filter swell across the eight bars; DARK ORCH enters on bar 5, low and measured. Both voices with a ghost harmony in fifths; the spectral choir takes the last line (four copies). The E pedal holds. Industrial drop #2 on bar 5, beat 1. Power moment: the last line.

### [The Sand]
*Bars 45–50 · 2:15–2:34 · energy 5→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — a fast, bright, high-register tower with no low end, then it collapses like sand)

- **Cue:** The shortcut. Chords: Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9 with **no E pedal**. MOON plays a bright, fast arpeggio in the highest register; the hats go fast and a crash falls on every bar; HARP stabs, thin and bright. No sub, no upright, no kick: all height and no foundation. Bars 1–4: the tower climbs faster and brighter. Bars 5–6: the collapse (C5 variant) — the arpeggio freezes into a grain cloud (Gross Beat time-hold), then scatters downward in pitch like sand until nothing is left.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar:         1       2        3        4        5                   6
ARPEGGIO:    mid     higher   higher   TOP      [FREEZE → grains]   [SCATTER downward → nothing]
HATS:        fast    fast     fast     FAST     .                   .
SUB/UPRIGHT/KICK:   NONE — no foundation, E pedal removed
HARP:        stab    stab     stab     stab     .                   .
```

### [Solo — The Rebuild]
*Bars 51–58 · 2:34–2:58 · energy 2→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — rebuilt from the ground up, slower and surer, in the same order as the first build)

- **Cue:** The rebuild, in the **same order, slower**. Bars 1–2: SUB alone, one long E. Bar 3: UPRIGHT returns and CELLO solos, low, tender and slow. Bar 5: the E pedal returns at full strength, kick and rim re-enter and HARP holds mid-register notes; the solo rises through the registers and wails. MOON and DARK ORCH from bar 6; the spectral choir from bar 7. Bar 8: Industrial drop #3 — the keystone. Chords: Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11/E | Em7♭9 | Dm7/E, twice.

### [Final Chorus — The Cathedral]
*Bars 59–66 · 2:58–3:23 · energy 10 █*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — *it will outlast us.* Both voices on the hook with a third voice of ghost harmony in fifths. Ad-libs permitted here only.)

- **Cue:** **The cathedral.** Every register at once, and nothing hurried: each chord lasts two bars (Em7♭9, Fmaj7♯11/E, Cmaj7/E, Dm7/E). Full kit, steady and sturdy, crash every two bars; SUB fully open and the E pedal never moving; CELLO in its cry register, a steady held line; DARK ORCH sweeps slowly across all registers; the spectral choir at full width; CHOIR PAD swells under each chord change for the first time — the reserved lift: **the full-range strings block, wide but unhurried**. Ad-libs permitted here only.

### [Wave Tag — Settling]
*Bars 67–70 · 3:23–3:35 · energy 10→6 ▅*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the choir sustains E and B as the wave settles)

- **Cue:** No lead vocals; the spectral choir sustains the high E and B. The drums drop out and the wave slowly settles around Em7♭9 over the pedal. One industrial drop on bar 1 only.

### [Outro — Bedrock]
*Bars 71–74 · 3:35–3:48 · energy 3→1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the upright alone on the open fifth E – B)

- **Cue:** Everything falls away across four bars until only **the bottom** is left: UPRIGHT alone, long and ringing, on the open fifth E – B, with SUB under it. The last sound is the upright’s E ringing in the reverb. Bedrock.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar:        1        2        3        4  -> Silence
UPRIGHT:    E–B      E–B      E–B      E ringing ~~~~~  (open fifth, long)
SUB:        #        #        #        # ~~~
CHOIR/WAVE: [60%]    [30%]    .        .
EVERYTHING ELSE: leaves in reverse order of entry across bars 1–3 — the bottom is last
```
