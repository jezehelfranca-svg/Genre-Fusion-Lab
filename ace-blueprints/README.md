# ACE Production Blueprints — 10 Songs, One Sound World

Ten Production Blueprints, built with the ACE Songwriting framework, for the ten human-experience themes. **Every song uses the single master style below**; the per-theme genre labels from the brief (City Pop, indie-folk, synth-wave, gospel R&B and so on) are deliberately ignored. What changes from song to song is the *arrangement*: tempo, structure, who enters when, how the collapse happens, how the swell is built.

**Scope: arrangement only.** Each file follows the framework's six-section blueprint (Header & Style DNA · Song Map · Section-by-Section Arrangement · Full Production Score · Audio Alchemy Prompt · Hit Architecture Compliance) plus the FL Studio Implementation table. Vocal sections carry a one-line *lyric job* in place of lyrics, and the Bilingual Singback pillar is marked ⚠️ Deferred until lyrics are written.

## Master style (shared by all ten songs)

```
Bleak urban blues fusion, 70-90 BPM, dragging shuffle against sporadic industrial percussive drops, E minor Phrygian scales with jazz-blues extensions, spectral vocal warping creating city-wide refrains, expansive moonglow convolution reverb on all instruments, sudden structural collapse glitched disruptions, intimate solo sections swell into overwhelming waves of layered synths and wailing treated strings, raw male baritone and female contralto vocals, distorted electric cello played with a metal slide, heavily processed harmonica, deep sub-bass from upright bass, melancholic and theatrically despairing.
```

Section 5 of each blueprint maps this onto the 7-layer Audio Alchemy order with the song’s exact BPM and a short song-specific tail (under 1,000 characters, no artist names).

## What every song shares, and what each one does differently

Every song has the same four ingredients — a dragging shuffle (or a deliberate break from it), sporadic industrial drops, one structural collapse, and one intimate solo that swells into a wave — plus exactly one **reserved lift** (the single escalation saved for the last third) and one signature device.

| # | Signature device | Collapse | Reserved lift |
|---|------------------|----------|---------------|
| 1 | The Ticker: a ledger-click that doubles each section (1 → 2 → 4 → 8 per bar) | C1 tape-stop | The full spectral-choir block, Final Chorus |
| 2 | The heirloom motif passed through four instruments; shadow taps that grow darker | C5 granular freeze | The roof: baritone an octave below the contralto, plus CHOIR PAD |
| 3 | Each industrial hit is followed by a bit-crushed comedown; sidechain pump | C3 bit-crush dropout, then a C4 cut at the solo’s peak | Withheld — the stripped final chorus *is* the lift |
| 4 | An unchanging two-bar upright ostinato under seven stacking layers | C2 buffer-stutter | The seventh layer (spectral choir) in the Final Chorus |
| 5 | Call-and-response; the voice count grows 1 → 2 → 4 → 8+; an unresolved E7♭9 | C4 vacuum cut (“winter field”) | The full backing-vocal congregation |
| 6 | A unison Stab figure that cracks; the only bone-dry break on the record | C3 dropout into the dry room, then a C4 cut | The full DARK ORCH block returning with the stabs |
| 7 | The 12-bar blues, bandwidth, low end and refrain all shrinking | C3 phone-speaker crush | The full-bandwidth restoration in the solo |
| 8 | A stomping march; a spoken ledger stamped after each entry; ends on a bare fifth | C4 vacuum cut | Tenths instead of octaves, plus CHOIR PAD |
| 9 | Built bottom-up over an E pedal; a tower of “sand” collapses | C5 variant: grains scatter downward | The full-range strings block, unhurried |
| 10 | One four-bar loop with a chain rattle; the snap; a straight beat; E major | C4 vacuum cut + chain-break hit | The mode change: B7 → E major |

**G♯ (the major third) is rationed.** It only *flashes* — the borrowed bright note and an E7♭9 turnaround in song 1, a brief E7♭9 in song 3, the polite E7♯9 cadence in song 6, and in song 5 a hanging E7♭9 and a final lone G♯ — and is never allowed to settle. The first time a G♯ stays, over a full E major chord, is song 10. Songs 2, 4, 7, 8 and 9 never use it.

## Shared vocabulary

- **Bar:** one 4/4 bar of shuffle (12/8 feel) at the song’s BPM. Rhythm maps use 12 triplet slots per bar; the legend is at the top of each Section 3.
- **Energy** runs 1–10 (1 near silence · 3 intimate · 5 groove · 7 full band · 9–10 the overwhelming wave), shown as `▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█`; `3→8` means the section rises.
- **Industrial drop:** a HAZARD strike (Toxic → Industrial Hazard ToTc) with a BOMB boom (SubWoofer Bomb ToTc) and Metallic SPYRO hits, followed by a short gap of silence. The kind varies by song (pipe, anvil, gavel, shutter, chain) and is never on a regular grid.
- **Spectral choir** (the “city-wide refrain”): the lead vocal copied onto three or more lanes, Frequency Shifter / Vocodex at +12, −12 and +7 semitones, delayed ½, 1 and 2 beats, each tap darker, into the Convolver.
- **Collapse types:** **C1** tape-stop (Gross Beat) · **C2** buffer-stutter (Gross Beat, 1 beat → ½ → ¼ → ⅛) · **C3** bit-crush dropout (Fruity Squeeze) · **C4** vacuum cut (reversed Convolver swell, then a hard cut to silence and tail) · **C5** granular freeze (Gross Beat time-hold).
- **The swell** (intimate solo → overwhelming wave) is built by adding one layer every one or two bars: drums return → MOON pad → CELLO opens to its cry register → DARK ORCH wails → spectral choir → a final industrial drop on the peak.
- **Instrument key:** UPRIGHT, SUB, CELLO, HARP, STALKER, MOON, DARK ORCH, CHOIR PAD, HAZARD, BOMB, SLOW BEAT and METALLIC are expanded to exact presets at the top of each Section 3.

## Using these

1. **Suno / Udio / ACE-Step:** paste Section 5’s bracketed prompt into the style field. The structural metatags below it go in the lyrics field, which still needs lyrics.
2. **FL Studio:** follow the FL Studio Implementation table at the end of each file and the rhythm maps in Section 3.
3. **Next steps in the framework:** `/ace-write` to add lyrics (the lyric jobs are written to slot straight in), `/ace-audit` to review them, `/audio-alchemy` to refine a prompt, `/album-generator` for cover art.
