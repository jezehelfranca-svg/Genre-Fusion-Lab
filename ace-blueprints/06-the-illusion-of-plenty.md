# Production Blueprint: The Illusion of Plenty (Facade Fusion Noir)

*Pretense & Keeping Up Appearances — 88 BPM · ≈ 3:38 · 80 bars · first chorus at 0:55*

## 1. Header & Style DNA
- **Title:** The Illusion of Plenty
- **ACE Layer 4 Context:** A facade. The first third is the most polished, expensive-sounding arrangement of the ten: crisp unison stabs, jazz-extended chords, a tight popped upright, a flawless shuffle. Then the cracks arrive one section at a time — a stutter, a missing stab, a one-bar dropout — until the centrepiece, a four-bar break where **the moonglow reverb is removed entirely**. It is the only time in the album, and it is meant to feel like air pressure dropping: the baritone is suddenly alone in a small, dry, empty room.

  I want the facade to be a real arrangement, not a parody, so it is built on strict register discipline: the Stab figure is one block (cello, strings, upright and a gated synth pluck) moving in unison, and nothing else is allowed to share its rhythm. The solo rebuilds the reverb bar by bar, the facade returns louder than ever, and then it fails again. The reserved escalation is that return: the full strings block alongside the stabs in the final chorus.
- **Production Style DNA:** bleak urban blues fusion, 88 BPM grid-tight polished shuffle, E Phrygian with jazz-blues extensions, unison cello and string stabs, popped upright and sub-bass, smooth sly baritone, glittering contralto, bone-dry break with all reverb removed, wailing strings, moonglow convolution reverb

## 2. Song Map Table

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Key** | E Phrygian (home chord Em7♭9). Jazz extensions (♯11, ♭9, 11) dress the minor chords; the polite Bm7♭5 → E7♯9 cadence is the facade’s only ‘happy’ sound — which is why it flashes the G♯. |
| **Tempo / Rhythmic Feel** | 88 BPM. The tightest shuffle in the set: grid-accurate to ~5 ms, slick and polished. The drag is hidden until the cracks show. |
| **Main Engine** | Bedrock: UPRIGHT = Morphine → BAS Upright Bass NUC; SUB = GMS → Pure Bass; CELLO = Sakura → STR Cello FG (Fruity Blood Overdrive → Distructor, metal-slide glissandi); HARP = recorded harmonica (Fruity Squeeze → Frequency Shifter). UPRIGHT pops and snaps; CELLO, DARK ORCH staccato hits and a gated MOON pluck combine as the Stab figure. |
| **Pads & Textures** | Bed and swell: STALKER = Morphine → ATM Stalker; MOON = GMS → Moon Landing TE; DARK ORCH = FLEX → Dark Orchestra by UVI; CHOIR PAD = Morphine → PAD Catholic Choir MC. DARK ORCH gives the staccato stabs and, later, the wail; MOON is gated for chord plucks and open for the wave; STALKER is barely used — the facade has no shadow; CHOIR PAD appears only in the wave tag. |
| **Drums** | Kit: SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx → Slow Beat MC (shuffle body, re-gridded to triplets); METALLIC = Drumaxx → Metallic SPYRO and Industrial 01–06 patterns; drops add Toxic Biohazard → Industrial Hazard ToTc (HAZARD, the strike) and SubWoofer Bomb ToTc (BOMB, the boom). Hit character: crisp and bright, brushed hats; the Industrial drops are chime-like — a clean metallic ring. |
| **Vocal Treatment** | **Smooth Lounge Confessional → Bare Whisper.** Baritone sly, mono, close, ValhallaVintageVerb ~6% (short and bright); contralto 20% left, glittering, then flat. Convolver and Valhalla sends are **all muted** in the Break and automated back across the Solo. The spectral choir is bright and clean in Chorus 1 and the Solo only. Designed power moments: Chorus 2 bar 5 and the Final Chorus’s held high E. Ad-libs only in the Final Chorus. |
| **Clarity Strategy** | Vocal Void (2–4 kHz) stays open: Maximus’ 2–4 kHz band on CELLO, HARP, DARK ORCH and MOON is keyed from the vocal bus (−3 to −5 dB while the voice sings). Register map: SUB 30–60 Hz · UPRIGHT 60–200 Hz · CELLO growl 80–250 Hz, cry 600 Hz–1.5 kHz · HARP 400 Hz–1.8 kHz · STALKER 150–800 Hz · CHOIR PAD 300 Hz–1 kHz · MOON and strings air above 5 kHz. Two parts never share a register at the same moment — they alternate in time. The Stab figure’s staccato hits are notched at 2–4 kHz under the voice. The first half is brighter and drier than any other song, so the dry Break feels like a drop in air pressure. |

## 3. Section-by-Section Arrangement

> **Instrument key** (exact presets in the Song Map and in the FL Studio Implementation table): UPRIGHT = Morphine BAS Upright Bass NUC · SUB = GMS Pure Bass · CELLO = Sakura STR Cello FG · HARP = recorded harmonica · STALKER = Morphine ATM Stalker · MOON = GMS Moon Landing TE · DARK ORCH = FLEX Dark Orchestra by UVI · CHOIR PAD = Morphine PAD Catholic Choir MC · HAZARD = Toxic Industrial Hazard ToTc · BOMB = Toxic SubWoofer Bomb ToTc · SLOW BEAT = Drumaxx Slow Beat MC · METALLIC = Drumaxx Metallic SPYRO.
>
> **Grid:** one bar = 12 triplet slots (12/8 feel); swung eighths land on the `li` slot. `K` kick · `k` soft kick · `S` snare · `s` ghost snare · `R` rim-click · `T` low tom · `x` hat · `o` open hat · `g` ghost hat · `X` unison stab · `B` upright note · `b` soft upright · `#` sub · `~` sustain · `^` tick · `!` industrial hit · `.` rest. The whole kit sits behind the grid by the amount stated in the Song Map.

### [Break — The Dry Room]
*Bars 53–56 · 2:22–2:33 · energy 1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — ‘…it’s empty.’ Baritone whisper, close and dry; the contralto answers with a single dry note.)

- **Cue:** **C3 bit-crush dropout** across the last beat of the previous section — and then the reverb is **removed**: every send to the Convolver and ValhallaVintageVerb muted, bone-dry. No sub, no shuffle. UPRIGHT plucks alone, dry. The baritone whispers the first three notes of the cell and cannot reach the high E. The contralto answers with a single dry note. Em7♭9 held. This is the only dry room on the record.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text
Bar 0 (last beat of the previous section):  [C3 BIT-CRUSH DROPOUT]
Bars 1–4:     ALL REVERB SENDS (Convolver + ValhallaVintageVerb)  [MUTED — BONE-DRY]
UPRIGHT:      plucked alone, dry, one note per bar
SUB / DRUMS:  none
VOCAL:        baritone whisper (B – E – F ... cannot reach the high E) + one dry contralto note
```

### [Solo — The Reverb Returns]
*Bars 57–64 · 2:33–2:55 · energy 2→9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — slide cello starting dry; the reverb blooms back in and the facade rebuilds)

- **Cue:** CELLO solo, bare and close at first, **dry**, then wailing as the Convolver send automates from 0% to 100% across eight bars — the reverb’s return *is* the swell. SLOW BEAT re-enters on bar 5; the Stab figure re-enters in bar 5, flawless again and getting louder; MOON opens in bar 6, DARK ORCH wails from bar 6, a spectral choir from bar 7; HARP answers the cello in bars 3 and 4. Chords: Em7♭9 | Fmaj7♯11 | Cmaj9 | Dm9, twice.

- **Rhythm Map:**

### [Final Chorus — The Facade Rebuilt]
*Bars 65–72 · 2:55–3:16 · energy 9 ▇*

- **Lyrics:**
(Lyric job — *nothing’s wrong.* Both voices bright and forced, the hook held a beat too long. Ad-libs permitted here only.)

- **Cue:** The facade **rebuilt** — flawless, rigid, louder than anything before. Full kit, DARK ORCH wailing, the Stab figure perfect. The hook’s high E is held a beat too long — a smile held past its time. The reserved lift: the full DARK ORCH block returns alongside the Stab figure. Ad-libs permitted here only.

### [Wave Tag — Failure]
*Bars 73–76 · 3:16–3:27 · energy 10→1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the wave at maximum; the Stab figure misfires; vacuum cut)

- **Cue:** Bars 1–2: the wave at maximum — MOON, DARK ORCH and CHOIR PAD with the cello wailing at the top. Chords: Fmaj7♯11 | Fmaj7♯11 | Em7♭9 | Em7♭9. Bar 3: the Stab figure misfires and the stutters pile up. Bar 4: **C4 vacuum cut** — a reversed-reverb swell is sucked in, then hard silence.

- **Rhythm Map:**
```text

WAVE:          MAX     MAX     [STAB FIGURE MISFIRES; stutters pile up]    [C4 VACUUM CUT → HARD SILENCE]
```

### [Outro]
*Bars 77–80 · 3:27–3:38 · energy 1 ▁*

- **Lyrics:**
(Instrumental — the Stab figure plays once, flawless, quiet and bone-dry)

- **Cue:** UPRIGHT alone, dry. The Stab figure plays **once**, flawless and quiet and bone-dry — it sounds hollow, which is exactly what it is. Then room tone and a single drip. HARP plays B – E and stops short: the cell never completes. No reverb tail.
